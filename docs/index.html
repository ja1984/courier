Two keystrokes

From “Copy as cURL” to a response in a second.

Copy a request from your browser's dev tools, your docs or a Slack thread. Courier understands it, opens it in a tab and sends it.

  1. 1Press ⌘V anywhere. A cURL command on the clipboard becomes a new tab — headers, body, auth and all.
  2. 2Press ⌘↩. The response lands in a collapsible JSON tree with status, timing and size.
  3. 3Press ⌘S. Keep it in a collection. Collections are never trimmed like history.

Bring your Postman stuff

Import Postman v2.0/v2.1 collections — folders, auth, JSON, form and GraphQL bodies come along.

Responses you can actually read

JSON in a collapsible tree, rendered HTML, pretty-printed XML, and ⌘F search across all of it. Click the status for a timing waterfall (DNS, TCP, TLS, first byte), sizes, IPs, TLS details and the certificate. Redirects aren't hidden either: every hop is listed.

Fluent in cURL

Chrome & Safari “Copy as cURL”, multi-line, -H, -d, --json, -u, -F and more. And any request copies back out as cURL.

Cookies that behave like a browser

Log in once and stay logged in: cookies the server sets go into a cookie jar and come back on later requests, following domain, path and expiry rules, even through redirects. Edit or delete any of them per request, add your own, and see exactly what each response set.

Headers that autocomplete

Type “con” and pick Content-Type, then application/json. It learns your custom headers too, never your tokens.

Tabs that remember

Tabs come back when you reopen the app, and ⇧⌘T brings back the one you just closed.

History, grouped

The last 500 requests, by date or by host. Search, reopen, or promote any of them to a collection.

Light, Dark or Auto

Built for macOS 26 with Liquid Glass and Apple-style tabs. It looks like it belongs on your Mac — because it does.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Courier is open source under the MIT license. No paid tier, no trial, no account.

Will macOS let me open it?

Yes. Releases are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any app from the App Store. Download the DMG, drag Courier to Applications, done.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 26 or later — it's a universal app for both Apple silicon and Intel.

Can I move over from Postman?

Export your collection from Postman (v2.0 or v2.1) and use File › Import Collections… in Courier. Folders, auth, bodies and disabled params carry over; {{variables}} are kept as text.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your Mac, encrypted with AES-GCM: tabs, history, collections and cookies. The key lives in your Keychain. Exports leave secret values out unless you explicitly tick “Include secret values”.

Something's missing. Can I ask for it?

Please do — open an issue or send a pull request.
